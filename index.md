# MaiML Documents

Markdown editions of MaiML documents, in Japanese and English.
The operational guideline is revised continuously; this site carries its current draft.

| Document | English | Japanese | Version |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operational Guideline | [en/guideline.html](en/guideline.html) | [ja/guideline.html](ja/guideline.html) | 0.8.14 (Draft) |
| Manifesto | [en/manifesto.html](en/manifesto.html) | [ja/manifesto.html](ja/manifesto.html) | 1.0.0 (Draft) |

## Status

- The operational guideline is an **informative** document. It does not add conformity
  requirements to the MaiML standard text or XML schema. Its English edition is a
  **working translation that has not been independently approved**.
- The manifesto is a **non-normative**, stand-alone document and a **pre-approval draft**.
- The specification and its requirements are defined by JIS K 0200 and the MaiML schema.

name and SHA-256 of its source. The files are not edited here. See the
[repository README](https://github.com/MaiML-org/MaiML-Documents#readme) for how they are built.
